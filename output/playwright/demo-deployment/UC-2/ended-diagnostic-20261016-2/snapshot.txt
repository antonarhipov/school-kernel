Privacy and storage

Your workspace is held in memory. Private temporary kernel files are cleaned up. Complete identical inputs, results and successful verification may remain in a shared memory cache for up to one hour after computation, independently of your session, subject to eviction; a restart clears them.

Saved definitions stay on this origin, browser and device until removed or evicted. Other users of a shared browser can read them. Download is the durable copy.

Railway edge logs include IP address, user agent and URL and cannot be disabled or redacted. Hobby viewing lasts seven days; provider staff report 90-day underlying storage, deletion afterward, in US West even for EU services.

Only the necessary application session cookie is used; application logs exclude school data. Do not submit personal data you are not permitted to share. Keep documents and personal details out of URLs.
