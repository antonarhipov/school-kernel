## Findings

### C-1 CRITICAL - Focused resource comparison loses the other side

`spec.md:413-416` requires administrator investigation/focused context with exact accepted and proposed sides; `spec.md:164-166` and RULE-5 (`rules.md:153-158`) require one-sided investigation to retain both needed representations. `A:703-705` passes accepted/proposed representations to `F:4-8`, which filters *each representation* by its own focused `teacherId`/`roomId`, not the joined lesson ID. For a changed teacher or room, focusing the accepted resource shows only the origin; focusing the proposed resource shows only the destination. Independently invoking this exact shipped renderer with both sides of one lesson produced `old teacher origin/destination true false` and `old room origin/destination true false`; `B:1900-1903` tests only a class focus for a period move and cannot catch this. The primary comparison representation and its other side disappear from that drill-down. Revision outcome: make focused old/new resource investigations retain the lesson's comparison context with honest side cues and demonstrate both focus directions in a real browser on a changed teacher and room, without duplicate unique totals.

### G-1 GAP - Normative proposal shapes do not consume a verified candidate

`spec.md:398-401,472-474,548-563` requires the UC-3 verified feasible proposal and normative full-school conditions. RULE-9 (`rules.md:211-220`) says “verify the accepted pair before use”; RULE-12 (`rules.md:251-258`) says “MUST begin UC-4 with UC-3's independently verified feasible proposal and assert every combined-overlay shape”. `B:1313-1371` does prove a real small packaged repair and `B:1387-1427` proves its durable acceptance. But `B:1859-1864,1923-1926,1977-2009,2244-2308` constructs review/result with placeholder revisions, uses `reviews.create` as its own oracle and directly writes `REPAIR_PROPOSAL` into JDBC. Such a candidate was not produced or verified through UC-3; the normative-size accepted pair verified in `B:2206-2241` is not used for this candidate. These browser tests prove conditional rendering of injected input, not the required production handoff for all shapes/scale and exact normative data. Revision outcome: produce/verify a feasible candidate at the real run or verifier boundary on an independently value-checked normative fixture and use it for boundary assertions of overlay shapes, direct/ripple/protection, and both decision paths; synthetic UI-only edge cases may supplement but not replace it.

### G-2 GAP - Exact comparison and availability assertions are incomplete

`spec.md:410-416,425-439,454-465,472-479`, RULE-5 (`rules.md:153-162`), RULE-6 (`rules.md:167-176`), and RULE-12 (`rules.md:251-258`) demand exact unique-ID/category/group/protection totals, every named before/after dimension, one-sided subject/teacher/search/filter matching, availability from the proper definition, and retained representable context. `B:1883-1908,1940-1962` asserts substrings `100`/`103`, `50` direct/`50` ripple in the 100-change fixture, one zero category, one `forcedMoves`, two period labels and a room-name fallback; it does not compare complete independently expected ID/side sets, all six category/group values, exact all-field before/after sides, teacher/subject/search-only matches or accepted-versus-proposed availability values. `B:1411-1416` checks Week after accepting from Week, not a retained nondefault Day/focused selection. `ProposalReviewService.java:28-97` and `A:195-216,619-628,1001-1013` make the desired behavior plausible, but a wrong field, swapped category or omitted side could still pass these assertions. Revision outcome: add independently computed value-by-value browser/DB checks for all named fields, overlapping and zero category/group membership, both availability definitions, each one-sided investigation type and a nondefault representable accepted range after confirmed acceptance.
